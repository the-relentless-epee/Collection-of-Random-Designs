# Arguably best filament holder for Cereal box Dry Boxes
This design uses a center roller for the filament spool, compared to most other dry box designs that use 2 bottom rollers to hold the outer edge of the spool.

This design has several advantages: 
1. less friction, requires less force from the extruder to pull the filament. Especially useful for TPU.
2. Works with different spool sizes. With bottom rollers, the contact angle between the rollers and the spool changes depending on spool diameter. Sometimes doesn't work with smaller spools. The center roller design works with spools of almost all sizes. (Designed for Bambu spools, tested with Eryone and Overture)
3. Works with cardboard spools. Bottom rollers often lead to extra wear on the outer edges of the spool. Not great for cardboard spools, especially ones that are slightly bent from shipping. This design works with cardboard spools better. (It is recommended to print a "bushing" for cardboard spools that aren't resin coated at the edges. Takes less than 1g of filament)
4. Less filament used: this design uses significantly less material compared to bottom roller designs.

Compatibility:
There are two models of the most common cereal boxes: ones with 3 small dents, and ones with 1 deep dent. The small dents version is better because the inner dimension is wider. From my testing, the version with 1 deep dent doesn't fit Bambu spools (55mm wide)
They are probably available on your local Amazon, make sure you have the right version.

Compatible with almost all 0.5/0.75/1kg spools.
